What does the store review cover?

The exact checklist depends on your platform, catalog size, and the issues found on your store.

Keyword research and search intent

Find relevant queries for products and categories and align important pages with clear search intent.

Product and category pages

Review titles, descriptions, content, category organization, and internal linking.

Crawling and indexing

Check sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, and important page signals in Search Console.

URLs and redirects

Review old and broken URLs and changes to product or category addresses when needed.

Images and mobile experience

Review image alt text, file size, mobile browsing, and page performance signals.

Structured data and measurement

Check relevant product data and available Google measurement signals without adding duplicate or misleading markup.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with only one ecommerce platform?

My current focus is Salla, Zid, and Shopify stores. Review steps depend on each store's setup and available data.

Are SEO steps different across platforms?

Yes. Some platforms generate sitemaps or structured data automatically, while other tasks depend on admin settings, themes, apps, extensions, or custom development. The actual store setup should be checked first.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No specific Google ranking can be guaranteed. The focus is on improving page quality and technical foundations, then measuring progress against available data and competition.

What do you need to start?

Send the store URL, platform name, target market, and your main goal. Search Console data is helpful when available, but a first review of public pages can begin without it.
