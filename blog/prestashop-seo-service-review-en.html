SEO Services: What a Useful Store Review Should Include

A practical, platform-aware guide based on official documentation and store-level SEO principles.

November 2, 2026By Mohamed AdamTarget topic: PrestaShop SEO services

Hiring an SEO specialist should buy a transparent diagnosis and prioritized work, not a promise of a specific Google position. A useful review connects technical access, page quality, search intent, and the store's commercial goals.

1. Start with the business objective

Decide whether the priority is qualified organic visits, product discovery, category visibility, local or international reach, or more enquiries and sales. The target country and language change the keyword set and the competitors that matter.

2. What should the SEO review include?

  • Keyword and intent mapping: assign each query to a product, category, brand, or article.
  • On-page review: unique titles, descriptions, headings, useful product details, image alt text, and internal links.
  • Technical checks: crawl access, index directives, canonical URLs, sitemap, redirects, mobile usability, and speed.
  • Measurement: Search Console, analytics, enquiries, add-to-cart actions, and sales where tracking is reliable.

3. What is specific to PrestaShop?

PrestaShop's official help center covers product SEO fields such as meta titles, descriptions, and simplified URLs, and recommends unique titles and original product descriptions. Its URL documentation covers friendly URLs and handling old URLs with suitable redirects. Check the current version, theme, and modules before implementation.

The exact controls can vary by version, theme, app, plan, or deployment. Ask the specialist to inspect your live dashboard and demonstrate the available fields before recommending bulk changes.

4. Questions to ask before hiring

  • Which URLs and keyword groups will be prioritized?
  • Which tasks are recommendations and which are implementation?
  • How will technical changes be tested and rolled back?
  • What access is needed, and how will it be protected?
  • What will the report show, and how often?

5. Measure progress without unrealistic promises

Record a baseline for impressions, clicks, query groups, indexed priority URLs, organic enquiries, and sales. Compare like-for-like periods and annotate promotions, seasonality, and major releases. Rankings fluctuate; a credible service explains uncertainty and reports outcomes rather than guaranteeing a fixed position.

Before approving a proposal
  • Target market and language are explicit.
  • Priority pages and deliverables are listed.
  • Technical, content, and measurement tasks are separated.
  • Risks, dependencies, and access requirements are clear.

A good PrestaShop SEO service is specific to the actual store, documented, and tied to customer needs. It should leave the merchant with a clear explanation of what changed and how success will be evaluated.
