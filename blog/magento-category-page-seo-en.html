Category SEO: Build Useful Landing Pages for Shoppers

A practical, platform-aware guide based on official documentation and store-level SEO principles.

November 2, 2026By Mohamed AdamTarget topic: Adobe Commerce (Magento) category SEO

A category page can satisfy a broader shopping need than one product URL. It should explain the product range, help shoppers compare options, and provide a clean path to relevant products. A thin archive or a near-duplicate category rarely adds value.

1. Build categories around real shopping needs

Group products by type, use, audience, or a meaningful attribute. Avoid creating a separate category for every close synonym if the inventory and search intent are the same. Make the hierarchy understandable to both shoppers and crawlers.

2. Give each category a distinct search focus

Choose one primary topic for the category and supporting terms that describe its products. Write a unique title and description; do not copy one template across all categories. Keep the visible heading consistent with the category's actual contents.

3. Add helpful category copy

Explain what belongs in the group, how the products differ, and what a buyer should compare. A short introduction can be enough when it answers a real question. Avoid generic paragraphs that could be pasted unchanged onto any store.

4. Connect categories and products

  • Link parent categories to important subcategories.
  • Link product pages back to their main category.
  • Use breadcrumbs when the theme supports them.
  • Link relevant guides to the category and featured products.

5. Manage filters and duplicate URLs

Filters and sorting help shoppers, but may create many URL variants. Decide which filtered combinations deserve a dedicated landing page. Review canonical, noindex, and crawl behavior using the platform's documented controls. Do not accidentally block the main category.

6. Platform considerations

Adobe Commerce documents SEO tools including metadata, XML sitemaps, URL rewrites for products, categories, and CMS pages, and crawler instructions. Some controls are documented for PaaS or on-premises deployments and do not automatically apply to Cloud Service or headless storefronts.

Category review
  • Does this page serve a distinct search or shopping need?
  • Is its title unique and accurate?
  • Does its copy help a customer choose?
  • Are products and subcategories linked logically?
  • Are filter URLs controlled?

Effective Adobe Commerce (Magento) category SEO earns visibility by being useful, distinct, and connected to the rest of the catalog.
