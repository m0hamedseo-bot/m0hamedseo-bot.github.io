Shopify Keyword Research and Content Plan: Map Queries to the Right Pages

A useful keyword plan does not place every term on every page. It assigns each search need to the page type that can satisfy it best, then connects related pages into a clear shopping journey.

Published November 2, 2026By Mohamed AdamPrimary topic: Shopify keyword research

Keyword research for an online store should begin with products, categories, support questions, and the language customers use. Search volume is only one input; relevance and the ability of a page to satisfy intent matter just as much.

1. Collect real customer language

Use Search Console queries, site-search terms, customer emails, support conversations, product reviews, and competitor page headings. Separate branded terms, product terms, category terms, comparison searches, and how-to questions. Record the country and language for every data set.

2. Map intent to page type

  • Transactional: a specific product name or model belongs on a product page.
  • Commercial research: a comparison or product-group query may fit a category or buying guide.
  • Informational: a how-to or troubleshooting question belongs in an article or help page.
  • Navigational: a search for the store or a known brand should lead to its intended destination.

For Shopify, consider the platform's content types and editable fields before choosing where a keyword should live. Start with product and collection terms, then add comparison and usage questions. Link a blog post to the collection it explains, and link the collection to relevant products. Shopify lets merchants edit blog-post SEO too, so let articles target informational questions while collections and products target commercial or transactional searches.

3. Create a focused content calendar

Prioritize a small group of topics that support important products or categories. A useful article should answer a real question, include practical steps or comparisons, and point to a relevant product or category when appropriate. Do not publish near-identical articles for every synonym.

4. Build useful internal links

Link from a guide to the category or product that it helps the reader choose. Link from category pages to relevant guides when they clarify differences or answer common questions. Use descriptive anchor text and avoid forcing an exact-match keyword into every link.

5. Measure business outcomes

Track impressions, clicks, query groups, landing pages, add-to-cart actions, enquiries, and purchases where analytics can measure them reliably. Review which pages earn impressions without clicks, and which attract visits that do not progress toward a purchase. Update the page that best fits the intent rather than creating another overlapping URL.

Keyword map columns:
  • Query and language
  • Country or target market
  • Intent
  • Primary destination URL
  • Supporting terms
  • Next action and success metric

A focused Shopify content plan connects search demand to helpful pages and meaningful actions. It avoids arbitrary keyword density and gives every new article a clear role in the store's customer journey.
