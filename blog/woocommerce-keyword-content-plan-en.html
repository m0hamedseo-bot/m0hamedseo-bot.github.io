Keyword Research and Content Planning: Map Intent to the Right Page

A practical, platform-aware guide based on official documentation and store-level SEO principles.

November 2, 2026By Mohamed AdamTarget topic: WooCommerce ecommerce keyword research

Keyword research for an online store is useful only when it changes what page you create or improve. Search volume is one input; intent, relevance, competition, and the store's ability to satisfy the query also matter.

1. Collect real customer language

Use Search Console queries, internal site search, support tickets, customer reviews, sales conversations, and competitor landing pages. Record the country, language, date, and source for each data set. Separate brand, product, category, comparison, and how-to queries.

2. Map search intent to a page type

  • Transactional: a specific model or item belongs on a product page.
  • Commercial investigation: a comparison or group query may fit a category or buying guide.
  • Informational: a how-to question belongs in an article or help page.
  • Navigational: a store or brand query should reach its intended destination.

3. Build a focused content calendar

Prioritize subjects that support important products or categories. Each article should answer a real question with practical steps, examples, or a comparison. Avoid publishing near-identical posts for every wording variation.

4. Use platform-aware content types

WooCommerce runs on WordPress, so permalink and product-taxonomy behavior interacts with WordPress settings, the active theme, and the chosen SEO plugin. WooCommerce documents product, category, tag, and attribute permalink settings. Core product structured data may also be extended by themes or plugins.

Before assigning a keyword, confirm which page types and editable fields your current platform setup supports. A product page, category archive, CMS article, and blog post are not interchangeable.

5. Connect pages with internal links

Link a guide to the category or product it helps a reader choose. Link from categories to guides that explain important differences. Use descriptive anchor text naturally; do not force an exact-match phrase into every link.

6. Measure outcomes and update the map

Track impressions, clicks, query groups, landing pages, enquiries, add-to-cart actions, and purchases where measurement is reliable. Identify pages that receive impressions without clicks and pages that attract visits without meaningful next steps. Improve the best-fit destination instead of creating an overlapping URL.

Keyword map fields
  • Query, language, and country
  • Search intent and funnel stage
  • Primary target URL
  • Supporting terms and questions
  • Official or first-party evidence
  • Success metric and review date

A practical WooCommerce content plan assigns every keyword a clear role in the customer journey and uses evidence to decide what to publish next.
